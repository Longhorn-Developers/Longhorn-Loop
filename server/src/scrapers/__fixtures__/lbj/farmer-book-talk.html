SRH 3.122 | March 04 | 12:30PM - 2PM

 

Join the Center for the Study of Race and Democracy (CSRD) for a book talk featuring historian and author Ashley Farmer. Through a discussion of her scholarship and latest work, the event will explore themes of Black history, activism, and the ongoing pursuit of social justice. Attendees will have the opportunity to engage with Farmer’s insights through a presentation and audience Q&A.

 

We invite students, faculty, and community members to join us for this meaningful conversation.
