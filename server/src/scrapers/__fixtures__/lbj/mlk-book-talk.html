SRH 3.122 | January 14 | 12:30PM - 2PM


Join the Center for the Study of Race and Democracy (CSRD) at the LBJ School of Public Affairs for an MLK Book Talk featuring Harvard Professor Brandon M. Terry and his book, Shattered Dreams of Infinite Hope: A Tragic Vision of the Civil Rights Movement.

Held in honor of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.’s enduring legacy, this event will explore Dr. King’s political thought, the complexities of the Civil Rights Movement, and the continued relevance of his vision for justice and democracy. The program will feature a presentation by Professor Terry, followed by a moderated discussion and audience Q&A.

We invite students, faculty, and community members to join us for this meaningful conversation.
