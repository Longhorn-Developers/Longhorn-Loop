Title: HRSA Grants Presentation

Date: Wednesday, October 21, 2026

Time: 12:00 PM – 1:00 PM CDT

Location: NUR 5.178 | School of Nursing | 1710 Red River St., Austin, Texas

Presented by: Jane Presenter, MSIS
Assistant Director, Cain Center for Nursing Research, UT Austin School of Nursing

Description: The Center for Professional Development and Scholarship invites you to a Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) Grants Presentation. 

This session will provide an overview of the grant-funding landscape and the proposal-submission process for nursing faculty. Topics will include federal and non-federal funding opportunities, proposal requirements, budget development and justification, and resources available to support faculty throughout the grant application process. Examples of funding opportunities, including HRSA grants, will also be discussed.

Contact: For questions, please email Jane Staffer at jane.staffer@example.edu.

Brought to you by: Center for Professional Development and Scholarship

About Center for Professional Development and Scholarship (CPDS) 

The Center for Professional Development and Scholarship (CPDS) in the School of Nursing serves as a hub to support faculty in pedagogical expertise and scholarly activities by assisting them in the planning, implementation, evaluation, and dissemination of innovative teaching strategies through manuscript authorship, educational grants, and professional presentations.
